Riley & Friends Beer Walk

An annual excuse to drink our way across Fishtown and Kensington.

Every summer, a shrinking-then-growing group of friends spends a day walking to as many breweries, cideries, and taprooms as we can reach on foot — one drink at each, try everything, try not to die. This is where we keep score.
