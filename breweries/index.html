Two years, thirteen stops

The Breweries

Every place we've hit across both walks, in one list. Ratings use our own scale — the Momentum Scale — because "was the beer good" only tells you half of whether a stop worked.

The Momentum Scale

Not a star rating. A rating of how each stop affects the pace of the walk — because on a route with twelve more stops to go, that matters as much as the beer.

Sprint Run Walk Crawl
Sprint — Itinerary-breaking. Good enough to abandon the schedule and just stay.
Run — The ideal pace-setter. One drink, good vibes, back on schedule.
Walk — Fine. Utilitarian. One quick pour, use the bathroom, move on.
Crawl — Momentum killer. Slow service, packed, no seats — threatens the whole day.

Ratings are added by hand as we get around to writing them up — unrated stops just haven't been logged yet, not judged.

  • Evil Genius Beer Co.

    Brewery · Fishtown · 1727 N Front St

    On the walk: 2026
    Walk Through 2026

    Beers are just okay. The inside is large — a great filler stop with outdoor seating too. Everyone can find a seat. One beer and move on.

  • Fermentery Form

    Brewery · Kensington · 1700 N Palethorp St

    On the walk: 2026
    Sprint Through 2026

    Awesome beer, great ambiance — feels like a back-alley bar in Belgium. Since it's a blendery and mostly farmhouse ales, it can't be a late stop, but starting here or hitting it early is a great way to cool off and check out all the barrels. Grab a bottle of Lofi or Petite and your group is good to go.

  • Forest & Main

    Brewery · Fishtown · 1416 Frankford Ave

    On the walk: 2026
    Walk Through 2026

    New for 2026 — the beers are really good. Can get busy, and it's hard to find a seat later at night. The pub lager is the move on the walk. I love their farmhouse ales, but stay away from those while walking — they'll slow you down. We ended up getting food here to stay alive.

  • Frankford Hall

    Beer Garden · Fishtown · 1210 Frankford Ave

    On the walk: 2025

    Not yet rated — hasn't made it into a write-up yet.

  • Human Robot

    Brewery · Kensington · 1710 N 5th St

    On the walk: 2025 & 2026
    Sprint Through 2026

    We've started the walk here both years — best lagers in Philly, by far. The Milk Tubes are a must. It's easy to get ahead of yourself because the beers are that good. Great vibes, and an amazing start to the day. Whenever anyone asks me what beer to try, Human Robot is the first name I bring up.

  • Lost Time Brewing Co.

    Brewery · Kensington · 2145 N Front St

    On the walk: 2025 & 2026
    Walk Through 2026

    Mikey's Gym. Grab the lager, or if you're feeling frisky, get the sour (not a big fan myself). In 2026 a walker grabbed a random stromboli from a vendor outside. Easy in and out. Not a big fan of dogs in the establishment, but overall a positive experience — a great neighborhood spot to spend some quality time.

  • Mural City Cellars

    Urban Winery · Fishtown · 1831 Frankford Ave

    On the walk: 2025 & 2026
    Crawl Through 2026

    We've come here both years, and both times we probably didn't need to end up here so close to the end of the walk. After drinking all day, arriving at a winery doesn't feel like an oasis — it feels like dread, because at that point you need water more than you need more fermented grapes.

  • Pip's

    Cider Bar · Fishtown · 1321 N Lee St

    On the walk: 2026
    Walk Through 2026

    New for 2026. Great vibes, but it's down near Frankford, which means it gets busy with cool people. The free pool table must pull folks in from the street. They're friends with Human Robot — one of the few places outside the taproom where you can get it on draft, not just in a can. They've also got some pretty cool funk ciders. Watch out, though: some of the drinks are high ABV, and in 2026 we got there later in the night, so it was hopping.

  • Punch Buggy Brewing Co.

    Brewery · Kensington · 1445 N American St

    On the walk: 2025 & 2026
    Walk Through 2026

    The beers are nothing to write home about, but it's on the way to other places and makes a great space-filler. Nice outdoor area when it's not 100 degrees. They've also got a PS5 — one walker this year skipped the beer entirely and just chilled on the console.

  • Sacred Vice Brewing Co.

    Brewery · Kensington · 120 W Berks St

    On the walk: 2025 & 2026
    Run Through 2026

    Easy to grab a beer. The lagers are pretty good — not as good as Human Robot, but the venue is awesome: vintage decor with a great wooden bar and plenty of taps. In 2026 we got there early and it was easy in and out. Get there later on a Saturday and it'll get busy, and potentially loud depending on who's spinning the vinyl — we found that out in 2025.

  • St. Oner's

    Brewpub — a Tired Hands joint · Fishtown · 2218 Frankford Ave

    On the walk: 2025 & 2026

    Not yet rated — hasn't made it into a write-up yet.

  • Wissahickon Brewing Co.

    Brewery · Kensington · 1526 N American St

    On the walk: 2025 & 2026
    Run Through 2026

    Great addition to American Street. The beers are okay, but the food comes out quick. You might have to fight through some dogs and babies in the fall, but midsummer on a Saturday it's smooth sailing. Grab some fries — helps you not die. In 2025 a few of us got bogged down by the alcoholic slushies. We avoided that mistake in 2026.
