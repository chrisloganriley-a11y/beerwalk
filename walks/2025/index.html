Fishtown & Kensington

Fishtown has more than 20 breweries, cideries, and taprooms packed into a few square miles — reason enough, we decided, to celebrate it with a walk. On the Fourth of July, 2025, we set out at noon in the middle of a Philadelphia summer to see how much of it we could cover in a day.

Turnout was great, and most of the group stuck it out for nearly the whole route. We finished the night with burgers at Johnny Brenda's.
